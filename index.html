About

I am an economist and researcher based in Hanoi. I work as a Research Assistant to Dr. Toan Phan (Federal Reserve Board) on projects at the intersection of climate risk and macroeconomics, and to Dr. Hien Pham on mechanism design theory. I am also a Research Assistant at DEPOCEN. My research interests span climate economics, macroeconomic theory, international trade, and applied econometrics.
